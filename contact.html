For recruiters & hiring teams

I am looking for roles in robotics, control, mechatronics, autonomous systems and machine learning. That covers navigation, motion planning and multi-robot coordination, control design from model predictive control to low-level controllers, state estimation and sensor fusion, computer vision and perception, reinforcement learning, and the modelling, system identification and optimisation work underneath all of it. I am equally at home on the hardware side, from embedded compute and electronics integration to field testing, and the same methods apply outside robotics wherever a system has to be modelled and decisions have to be made from limited data. I completed my MSc in Systems and Control at TU Delft in 2026, am based in Delft, and am happy to talk through my projects or code in a call.

For researchers

My research sits around learning-based control and formal guarantees, covering temporal-logic planning, symbolic control, safe multi-robot autonomy and statistical certification with PAC bounds, conformal prediction and the scenario approach. I am glad to discuss my papers, my thesis or a possible collaboration.
